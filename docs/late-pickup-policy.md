# Late Pickup and Supervised Waiting Area Policy (Draft)

**For:** School administration, guards and faculty on duty
**Status:** Draft for the school to review, adapt and adopt
**Works with:** SIMS Guard Portal → *Students on Campus*

## 1. Purpose

No student is ever left alone on campus after dismissal. Every student is either picked up by an authorized person, allowed to leave on their own (approved self-dismissal), or kept under the supervision of a named staff member until a parent arrives.

## 2. Late pickup time

A student still on campus after the **late pickup time** is a *late pickup*. The default is **5:00 PM**. The school administrator sets it in SIMS → System Settings → Pickup Rules.

## 3. Staff on duty

- Each school day, the principal assigns **one staff member on duty** for late pickups. That staff member may not leave until every waiting student has been released.
- If the assigned staff member is unavailable, the principal names a replacement before dismissal.

## 4. Supervised waiting area

- **Location:** one fixed, indoor, well-lit room near the main gate (to be named by the school, e.g. the faculty room or guidance office), with a working phone signal.
- **Supervision:** students in the waiting area are always within sight of the staff member on duty. No student waits alone, and no student waits alone with a single adult behind a closed door.
- **Staying put:** students may not leave the waiting area except with the staff member on duty, or when released to an authorized person at the gate.

## 5. Procedure at the late pickup time

| Step | Who | What | In SIMS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guard | Open *Students on Campus*. Students still present are flagged **LATE**. | Students on Campus |
| 2 | Guard | Mark students who were absent today. | **Absent** |
| 3 | Guard or staff on duty | Phone the parent, then the other contacts, in the order shown. | **Call list** → **Call** |
| 4 | Guard or staff on duty | Send the in-app notice. An email is also sent while the daily email limit allows. | **Notify parent** |
| 5 | Guard | Bring the student to the waiting area and hand them over to the staff member on duty, typing that person's full name. | **Hand over** |
| 6 | Guard | When the parent arrives, verify them as usual: QR code, photo and ID. | Scan QR Code |
| 7 | Guard | Once every student is picked up, handed over or marked absent, close the day. SIMS will not close the day while any student is unaccounted for. | **Close the day** |

## 6. If no one can be reached

- **30 minutes after the late pickup time:** keep calling every contact, and inform the principal.
- **60 minutes after:** the principal decides on the next step, for example a staff member taking the student home with a second adult present, or calling the barangay or the police. Follow the school's child-protection policy.
- Write down every call attempt and decision in the incident report.

## 7. Repeated late pickups

The guidance office contacts the family after **3 late pickups in one month** to agree on a plan, for example a second authorized guardian or a temporary pickup QR code for a relative.

## 8. Records and privacy

- SIMS keeps these records in the audit log: who handed a student over, to whom and when; every viewing of a call list; every notice sent; and who closed the day.
- Phone numbers are shown only on the call list, only to guards and office staff, and only for the student concerned (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

## 9. Review

The principal reviews this policy and the late-pickup records at the end of each grading period.
